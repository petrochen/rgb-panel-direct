RGB

The ENE RGB, NZXT Hue 2 and IT8297 RGB Fusion 2 protocol sequences are
adapted from OpenRGB 1.1, commit 8e16de27858cf2be6af54a33ce003df74cb9fd3e.
The application source is provided under the GNU GPL version 2 or later.
The licence is embedded as RgbPanel.GPL-2.0.txt.

OpenRGB project and original driver code:
https://github.com/CalcProgrammer1/OpenRGB/tree/8e16de27858cf2be6af54a33ce003df74cb9fd3e

The SmbusPIIX4 module bundled by OpenRGB is embedded byte-for-byte. The
installed PawnIO driver is accessed through its published device ABI.
PawnIO and PawnIO Modules sources:
https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO
https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Modules

The byte-identical signed module is also available in the PawnIO Modules
0.2.10 release. The LGPL-2.1-or-later corresponding source and license are
included as PawnIO-Modules-0.2.10-source.zip.
